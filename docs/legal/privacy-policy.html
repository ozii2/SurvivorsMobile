Privacy Policy — Ball Survive

Last updated: 10 July 2026
Developer: Oğuzhan Kandemir
Contact: oguzhan1kandemir@gmail.com
App: Ball Survive (com.oguzhan.ballsurvive)

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Ball Survive mobile game ("the App", "we", "us") handles, how it is used, and your choices. By installing or using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • The game works offline and requires no account or login.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • If you set a custom avatar, the picked image stays on your device.
  • We show ads via Google AdMob, which collects data such as your advertising identifier.
  • The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

2. Information we handle

On-device only (not sent to us): game progress, high scores, unlocked content, upgrades, sound/settings preferences, and an optional avatar image you choose from your gallery. You can erase this by clearing the App's data or uninstalling.

Collected by Google AdMob (advertising): advertising ID, device information (model, OS, approximate location derived from IP, network state), and ad interaction data — used to serve and measure ads. This is processed by Google under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases: the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We receive only confirmation of success — not your name, card number, or billing address.

3. Permissions

  • Photos / Media (gallery): requested only when you set a custom avatar; the image never leaves your device.
  • Internet: used to load ads. Gameplay does not require a connection.

The App does not access your microphone, contacts, precise GPS location, live camera, or SMS.

4. How data is used

On-device data runs the game and remembers your progress. Advertising data (by Google) shows relevant ads, limits ad frequency, measures performance, and prevents fraud. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal data, contact us and we will address it.

6. Data sharing

We do not share on-device data. Advertising and purchase data is processed by Google (AdMob / Google Play) as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Retention & deletion

On-device data persists until you clear the App data or uninstall. For any data request, email oguzhan1kandemir@gmail.com.

8. Your choices

You can reset or limit ad tracking in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). You can decline the gallery permission; the game remains fully playable.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes are reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
