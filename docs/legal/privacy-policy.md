# Privacy Policy — Ball Survive

**Last updated:** 10 July 2026
**Developer:** Oğuzhan Kandemir
**Contact:** oguzhan1kandemir@gmail.com
**App:** Ball Survive (package `com.oguzhan.ballsurvive`)

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Ball Survive mobile game
("the App", "we", "us") handles, how it is used, and your choices. By
installing or using the App, you agree to this policy.

---

## 1. Summary (plain language)

- The game itself works **offline** and does not require an account or login.
- Your **game progress and settings** are stored **only on your device**.
- If you set a **custom avatar**, the image you pick from your gallery stays
  **on your device** — we do not upload it anywhere.
- We show **ads** through Google AdMob. AdMob (Google) collects certain data
  such as your device's advertising identifier to serve and measure ads.
- If you buy the **"Remove Ads"** option, the purchase is processed by
  **Google Play**. We never see or store your payment details.

---

## 2. Information we handle

### 2.1 Data stored on your device only
The following is saved locally (device storage) and is **not** transmitted to us:
- Game progress, high scores, unlocked content, and upgrades
- Sound/settings preferences
- A profile avatar image, only if you choose to set one from your gallery

You can erase all of this by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

### 2.2 Data collected by third parties (advertising)
To keep the game free, we display ads via **Google AdMob**. Google may collect
and process data including:
- Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
- Device information (model, OS version, coarse/approximate location derived
  from IP, network state)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) for measurement and fraud prevention

This data is collected directly by Google under Google's own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 2.3 Purchases
The optional **"Remove Ads"** in-app purchase is handled entirely by
**Google Play Billing**. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase
succeeded — not your name, card number, or billing address.

## 3. Permissions the App requests

- **Photos / Media (gallery):** requested **only** when you choose to set a
  custom avatar. The selected image never leaves your device.
- **Internet / network access:** used to load ads. The gameplay does not
  require an internet connection.

The App does **not** access your microphone, contacts, precise GPS location,
camera (live), or SMS.

## 4. How the data is used

- Local data: to run the game and remember your progress and settings.
- Advertising data (by Google): to show relevant ads, limit ad frequency,
  measure ad performance, and prevent fraud.

We do **not** sell your personal data.

## 5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. Ads are configured to request
non-personalized/appropriate content where required. If you believe a child
has provided personal data, contact us and we will address it.

## 6. Data sharing

We do not share the on-device data with anyone. Advertising and purchase data
is processed by Google (AdMob / Google Play) as described above. We may
disclose information if required by law.

## 7. Data retention & deletion

On-device data persists until you clear the App data or uninstall the App.
To request any data-related action or ask a question, email
**oguzhan1kandemir@gmail.com**.

## 8. Your choices

- You can reset or limit ad tracking in your device settings
  (Settings → Google → Ads → Reset/Delete advertising ID, or opt out of
  ad personalization).
- You can decline the gallery permission; the game remains fully playable.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the
"Last updated" date above. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
